Scope Notes

What to expect

We want this to be completely clear before we start. Here's exactly what is and isn't covered so there are no surprises down the road.

What IS included

  • Posting any content, photos, or copy supplied by Open Arms
  • Content updates and additions for 12 months after launch

What is NOT included

  • Video editing beyond the 15 included interview videos

Video instructions included: We'll provide a simple guide so your team knows exactly how to record the interviews on an iPhone. We'll also recommend an optional wireless microphone for better audio quality.
