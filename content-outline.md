### OUR PHILOSOPHY

**Section Label:** Our Approach

**Headline:** Developing the Whole Child

**Intro paragraph:** We believe every child deserves to grow in heart, mind, body, and spirit — in a safe, predictable, and loving environment rooted in Christian values.

**Five Pillars (expandable accordion):**

**1. Academic Readiness**
Literacy, early mathematics, and science are woven into natural, hands-on activities. Children learn by doing — through field trips, experiments, and creative exploration that sparks genuine curiosity.

**2. Social-Emotional Growth**
Trained educators foster kindness, confidence, and curiosity. Predictable routines help children feel secure, and small class sizes ensure every child is truly known and celebrated.

**3. Spiritual Foundation**
Daily prayer, Bible stories, and weekly pastoral visits from Zion Lutheran nurture faith in every child. Our Christ-centered mission welcomes families of every background with open arms.

**4. Physical Activity**
Daily outdoor play and structured movement build motor skills and resilience — even in the Alaskan winter. We believe a healthy body supports a healthy mind.

**5. Creative Expression**
Art, music, and imaginative play are not extras — they are essential. We celebrate each child's unique gifts and encourage them to express who God made them to be.

### FAQ

**Section Label:** Common Questions

**Headline:** We're Here to Help

**Intro:** Can't find your answer? Give us a call or send us a message and we'll get right back to you.

---

**Q1: Is there currently a waitlist?**
Yes — due to high demand for quality childcare in Fairbanks, many of our programs maintain a waitlist. We encourage families to inquire as early as possible. Submitting an enrollment form above secures your place in line and our team will contact you as soon as a spot becomes available.

**Q2: Do I need to be Lutheran or Christian to enroll?**
Absolutely not. Open Arms welcomes families of every background, faith tradition, and walk of life. While our environment is rooted in Christian values — daily prayer, Bible stories, and character development — we serve the whole Fairbanks community, including military families, and students of all ethnicities and beliefs.

**Q3: What does NAEYC accreditation mean for my child?**
NAEYC (National Association for the Education of Young Children) accreditation is the gold standard in early childhood education. It means our curriculum, teacher qualifications, classroom ratios, health and safety practices, and family engagement all meet rigorous, research-based national standards — well beyond Alaska's basic state licensing requirements.

**Q4: What meals and snacks are provided?**
Open Arms participates in the USDA Food Program, which ensures all children receive nutritious, balanced meals throughout the day. The Before & After School program includes breakfast and afternoon snacks. Jovani, our Kitchen Manager, oversees daily meal preparation with care and consistency.

**Q5: How is the facility secured?**
Our facility features a secure keypad entry system at all entrances. Only authorized staff and pre-approved family members can access the building. This system, combined with our low student-teacher ratio of 8:1, ensures every child is accounted for and supervised at all times.

**Q6: What are the registration fees?**
The annual registration fee is $70 per child or $130 per family. This fee is non-refundable and is required at the time of enrollment to secure your placement. Monthly tuition rates vary by program — please contact our office for the current tuition schedule and to discuss subsidy eligibility.
