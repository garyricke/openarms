Where these submissions go

Every form submitted through the website is now being routed to adminassist@openarmsfairbanks.org. That single inbox receives notifications for:

  • Enrollment Inquiry — the short form on the landing page.
  • Waitlist Application — the full parent/guardian + child form on /waitlist.
  • Employment Application — the multi-step job application on /employment.

If you'd like different forms going to different email addresses — for example, sending employment applications to Tina and enrollment inquiries to the front desk — please email gary.ricke@orbisdesign.com with the routing you want and Gary will update Netlify.

Please test — staff messages

The staff "Message" buttons are a separate system. Please test every one.

On the landing page, each staff card has a Message button that opens a contact form. Those messages do not go to adminassist@. They are routed directly to that individual staff member's own email address using a different service (EmailJS). Because each card has its own destination, every card needs to be tested individually before we can trust the system.

What to do: visit the public site, scroll to the Our Staff section, and for each staff member with a Message button:

  • Click Message on the staff card.
  • Send a short test message (e.g., "Test from Open Arms team — please confirm receipt.").
  • Confirm with that staff member that the email arrived in their inbox — and that it didn't land in spam.
  • If anyone reports they did not receive the test, email gary.ricke@orbisdesign.com with the staff member's name and the email address it was supposed to go to. Gary will fix the routing.

Why this matters: the Message buttons are how families reach a specific teacher or administrator without an email address being exposed publicly on the site. If even one staff member's button is mis-routed (typo in the email address, missing entry, full inbox), messages meant for them will silently fail. A full top-to-bottom test of every card is the only way to verify the whole system before launch.

Note: Two staff members — Wesley and Paetynn — currently have no Message button shown because we don't have email addresses for them yet. If you'd like them included, please send their addresses to Gary.
